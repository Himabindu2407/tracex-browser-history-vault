Privacy controls

Pause recording or permanently delete TraceX's locally stored records.

TraceX records URLs and page titles using the browser history permission. It does not collect passwords, form contents or keystrokes. Data remains in this browser profile; uninstalling the extension or clearing its data can remove records. Incognito/private browsing is not captured in this build.
